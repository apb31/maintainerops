# Two bounded experiments

## CSV Rescue

[Use the free tool](https://ap-csv-rescue.angelcap.chatgpt.site)

An original, client-side CSV cleanup prototype: quoted-field parsing, exact-row deduplication, whitespace trimming, empty-row removal, a preview and downloadable CSV. Files remain in the browser tab. A scoped service enquiry goes directly to Ángel by email; no payment is taken on the page.

The first audience is people who need repeatable catalog imports, spreadsheet cleanup or data QA. Distribution starts with this real proof-of-work and relevant existing applications when the buyer requests an example. No bulk messages or advertising. Paid custom mapping, validation and cleanup require an agreed scope and quote. This is a demand test, with no claimed customers, revenue or conversion rate.

Validation gate: record an actual request, agree one small paid deliverable, then fulfill and reconcile payment. Expand only after a paid job or repeated independent requests. Stop additional speculative development until a useful demand signal arrives. Advertising budget: $0.

Checks performed: escaped quotes, embedded newlines, malformed quoted input, exact duplicate removal, empty rows, Unicode round-trip and optional formula-like-cell prefixing. Review the preview before using an export.

## SPARK GRID

[Play the fictional simulation](https://spark-grid-simulation.angelcap.chatgpt.site)

An original eight-round compute-dispatch game. SPK is an imaginary score: there is no blockchain, wallet, sale, cash redemption or return. Its named organizations, partnerships and 2041 story are explicitly fictional. The in-game brief contains hypothetical token allocations, clearly separated from implemented features.

The first test is whether a player wants another run and requests another level. Feedback is voluntary by email. Potential future revenue is a small game or cosmetic content, not token-price promises. No adoption, customers, demand or future valuation is asserted.

## Operations

These demos are deliberately bounded. Income delivery takes priority over extra features. Cash Ops Control tracks source evidence and verified stages; applications and advertised bounties do not count as income.
